About Raj Fruits

Raj Fruits is a trusted family-owned fruit business with over 40 years of experience serving customers in and around Courtallam and Tenkasi.


We provide premium fruits sourced from trusted growers and also from our own farms, ensuring freshness and quality from farm to customer.


We serve both wholesale and retail customers and regularly update our collection with seasonal fruits throughout the year.


Raj Fruits proudly operates in and around Courtallam and continues to serve local families, tourists, retailers, hotels and businesses.
