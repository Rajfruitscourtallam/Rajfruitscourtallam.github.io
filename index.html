About Raj Fruits

Raj Fruits is a trusted family-owned fruit business serving customers in and around Courtallam and Tenkasi for over 40 years.

We offer premium fruits sourced from trusted growers and from our own farms, ensuring freshness and quality from farm to customer.

We serve both wholesale and retail customers and regularly update our collection with fresh seasonal fruits throughout the year.

With two locations around Courtallam, Raj Fruits proudly serves local families, tourists, retailers, hotels and businesses.
