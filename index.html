About Raj Fruits

Raj Fruits is a trusted family-owned fruit business with more than 40 years of experience serving customers in and around Courtallam and Tenkasi.


We offer premium quality fruits from trusted growers and also from our own farms, ensuring freshness and quality from farm to customer.


We serve both wholesale and retail customers and regularly update our collection with fresh seasonal fruits throughout the year.


With two locations around Courtallam, Raj Fruits proudly serves local families, tourists, retailers, hotels and businesses.
